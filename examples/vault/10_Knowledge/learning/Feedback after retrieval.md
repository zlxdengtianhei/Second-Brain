## Essence
Retrieval helps even without feedback, but seeing the correct answer after a failed attempt lets the learner fix the error and makes retrieval work better.

## Propositions
- P1. Retrieval helps even without feedback.
- P2. Feedback makes retrieval work better.
- P3. After a failed attempt, seeing the correct answer lets the learner fix the error.
- P4. Flashcards work because each card is a small retrieval attempt followed by feedback.
