## Essence
Memory loss is fastest right after learning and then slows down, and this shape is called the forgetting curve.

## Propositions
- P1. Ebbinghaus learned lists of nonsense syllables in the 1880s and measured how much he could still recall after different delays.
- P2. Memory loss is fastest right after learning and slows down over time.
- P3. Much of what is lost goes in the first day, and what survives a week tends to survive much longer.
- P4. The exact rate depends on the material, on how meaningful it is and on how well it was learned in the first place.
- P5. Meaningful material is forgotten more slowly than nonsense syllables.

## Intuition and contrasts
- Intuition: The curve drops steeply at first and then flattens, so the early hours and days decide most of what is kept.
