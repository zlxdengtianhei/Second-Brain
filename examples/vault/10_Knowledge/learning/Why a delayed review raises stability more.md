## Essence
A review that comes after some forgetting has started forces a more effortful retrieval, and a successful effortful retrieval increases stability more than an easy one.

## Propositions
- P1. A review that comes after some forgetting has started forces a more effortful retrieval.
- P2. A successful effortful retrieval increases stability more than an easy retrieval.
- P3. This is offered as one explanation of the spacing effect, not as the only one.
