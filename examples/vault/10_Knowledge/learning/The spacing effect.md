## Essence
Studying material in several sessions spread over time gives better long-term retention than studying it for the same total time in one session.

## Propositions
- P1. Several sessions spread over time lead to better long-term retention than the same total study time in one session.
- P2. The spacing effect is one of the most often replicated findings in memory research.
- P3. Cramming the night before a test can give a good score the next morning, but much of that knowledge is gone a few weeks later.
- P4. Spaced study wins on any test given after a delay.
- P5. Massed study can win only on a test given right away.

## Intuition and contrasts
- Contrast: Cramming can look effective because a test the next morning rewards it; the advantage of spacing shows only on delayed tests.
