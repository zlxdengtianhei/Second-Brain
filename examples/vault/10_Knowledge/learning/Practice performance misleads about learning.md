## Essence
Measured during practice the easier methods look better, but measured after a delay the harder ones win, so judging a study method by how it feels in the moment leads to the wrong choice.

## Propositions
- P1. Measured during practice, the easier methods look better.
- P2. Measured after a delay, the harder methods win.
- P3. Judging a study method by how it feels in the moment leads to the wrong choice.
- P4. Examples in the source: rereading felt more confident but lost to recall after a week, and blocked practice scored better during practice but interleaving improved later performance.
