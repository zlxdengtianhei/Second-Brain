## Essence
Rereading makes a text feel familiar, and learners mistake that familiarity for knowing, although they can only recognise the material and cannot produce it unaided.

## Propositions
- P1. Rereading makes a text feel familiar.
- P2. Familiarity is easy to mistake for knowing: the learner recognises the material but cannot produce it unaided.
- P3. This mistake is called the illusion of fluency.
- P4. A simple check is to close the book and try to explain the idea or answer a question about it.
- P5. What cannot be recalled is not yet learned.

## Intuition and contrasts
- Intuition: Recognising a face is easier than drawing it from memory; the feeling of recognition hides the gap.
- Contrast: Recognition (the material feels familiar) is not the same as recall (producing it unaided); only recall shows learning.
