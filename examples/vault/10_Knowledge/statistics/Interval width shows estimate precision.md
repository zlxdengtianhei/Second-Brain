## Essence
The width of a confidence interval shows how precise an estimate is, and small samples or noisy data make it wider.

## Propositions
- P1. A narrower interval means a more precise estimate; a wider interval means a less precise one.
- P2. Smaller samples give wider intervals.
- P3. Noisier data give wider intervals.
