## Essence
Evidence changes a belief by how much more likely it is when the hypothesis is true than when it is false.

## Propositions
- P1. The rule says how much a piece of evidence should change a belief.
- P2. Evidence that is much more likely when H is true than when H is false raises the probability of H a lot.
- P3. Evidence that is about equally likely either way hardly changes the probability of H.

## Intuition and contrasts
- Intuition: Evidence is informative only when it separates the world where H is true from the world where H is false.
