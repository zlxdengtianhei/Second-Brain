## Task: independently review a taxonomy proposal

You did not write the proposal. Check the old taxonomy, the card list and the proposed taxonomy: clear boundaries, no one-card pillars, no synonym topics, wrong-domain cards are not hidden under new categories, the narrative explains the learning order, and existing categories are kept.

Output: `{"items": [{"domain", "decision", "reason"}]}`

- Cover every proposed domain exactly once; `decision` is `accept` or `revise` with a concrete reason. Do not use an `id` field and do not rewrite the proposal.
