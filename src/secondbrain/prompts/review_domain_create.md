## Task: independently review a new-domain proposal

You did not write the proposal. Check that the evidence set and the boundary hold (judge by card content, not titles), the taxonomy covers every migrated card without synonym pillars, new relation words are really needed, the difference from the current domain is real, and the impact is stated honestly. The question is whether the relation boundary is clear and the cards already form their own unit of knowledge, not whether the topic is large.

Output: `{"items": [{"domain", "decision", "reason"}]}` with exactly one item for the proposed domain and a concrete reason.
