## Task: say what came before a chunk of a source

A source is cut into chunks, and each chunk is turned into cards by itself. `before` is the source text just before the chunk; `chunk_start` is the chunk's beginning. In two or three sentences (at most `limit_chars` characters), say what the text before covers, and what the chunk's references point at ("the three kinds above", "this method", a symbol defined earlier). Write only what the text says; no judgement and no cards.

Answer `{"summary": "..."}`.
